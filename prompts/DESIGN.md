## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an emotionally protective, dignified, and contemplative sanctuary for personal prayer, audio reflection, and spiritual formation. Rejecting both clinical utility and performative sentimentality, the visual tone balances quiet reverence with modern warmth—evoking a candlelit room lined with natural linen, bound journals, and steady, restorative quietude.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Core Audience:** Believers seeking intentionality in their prayer lives, individuals walking through grief, burnout, or transition, and users prioritizing an unhurried, privacy-first audio experience over social feeds.
- **Emotional Signature:** Grounded, unburdened, safe, and reverent. The interface behaves like a respectful companion—patient, quiet, and deliberate.

### Design Movement: Tactile Minimal Sanctuary
The aesthetic blends tactile editorial minimalism with warm, organic surfaces:
- **Calm, Deliberate Air:** Generous, uncrowded negative space anchored by Warm Linen backdrops.
- **Editorial Reverence:** Bookish, literary typography for Scripture and prayers, supported by clean, empathetic interface controls.
- **Pure Materiality:** No artificial gradients, skeuomorphic gloss, or high-octane saturation. Depth is created through natural linen-tone shifts, whisper-soft borders, and delicate ambient shadows reminiscent of diffused, low-angled light.
- **Restraint Over Clutter:** Absolute avoidance of gamified badges, celebratory confetti, or emojis. Every action is grounded by line-drawn, single-stroke iconography.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around restorative natural tones, antique ink, and soft, organic washes. Color is applied purposefully to honor vulnerable emotions and elevate sacred text, never as decorative distraction.

### Functional Roles & Hierarchies
- **Primary (`#5B3A5E` - Deep Plum):** The primary focus token. Reserved for primary interactive levers (audio recording, active navigation tabs, major forward actions).
- **Secondary (`#D4A5A5` - Dusty Rose):** Strictly a gentle tint fill for subtle badge backings and soft wash highlights. Never used for body text or primary labels.
- **Text Accent (`#9E5A62` - Rosewood):** Dedicated to Scripture book and chapter references, active text links, and editorial highlights. Meets accessible contrast requirements over light surfaces.
- **Warm Accent (`#C4704F` - Terracotta):** Represents continuous practice, answered prayers, and encouraging milestones.
- **Calm Accent (`#8FA58A` - Sage):** Signifies completed synchronization, peace, and positive validation states.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FBF7F2` - Warm Linen): The resting background for full-screen flows.
  - Surface Tier 1 (`#F5ECE8` - Soft Blush): Scripture reflection cards, persistent banners, bottom sheets, and grouped audio trays.
  - Surface Tier 2 (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White): Floating cards, prayer journal tiles, and isolated modules.
- **Text & Borders:**
  - Ink Plum (`#2E2A33`): Deep, warm off-black for headlines, prompts, and primary body text.
  - Mauve Gray (`#8A7F88`): Secondary metadata, audio timestamps, and placeholder copy.
  - Petal (`#EADFDB`): Structural dividing lines, ghost card outlines, and input perimeters.
- **Critical / Recording:**
  - Crisis Red (`#B4443A`): Active mic recording indicators, audio deletion confirmation, and immediate crisis pastoral support access.

### Emotional State Tints
For journaling emotional check-ins, paired surface/text pairings preserve readability and mood dignity:
- **Anxious:** Background `#F3E6EE` | Text `#5B3A5E`
- **Grieving:** Background `#ECE6F0` | Text `#4A3B5C`
- **Grateful:** Background `#E8EFE6` | Text `#4F6B4A`
- **Hopeful:** Background `#F7E9DF` | Text `#9A4F31`
- **Weary:** Background `#F0EAE6` | Text `#6B5D57`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary dignity of `Newsreader` (standing in as the authoritative editorial serif for Scripture, prayer headings, and reflective prompts) with the uncluttered, human warmth of `DM Sans` for utility, transcription output, and interactive UI chrome.

### Typographic Guidelines
- **Scripture Verses:** Always rendered in `Newsreader` italic or roman (`scripture-quote`), set with deliberate line height (`32px`) to encourage slow reading and breath.
- **Audio Transcripts:** Set in `DM Sans` (`body-lg`) in `#2E2A33`. Utterances are grouped into breathable paragraphs rather than dense blocks.
- **Reference Citations:** Book, chapter, and verse references use `label-md` in `DM Sans` with Rosewood `#9E5A62` tinting.
- **Pacing & Breathing Room:** Never allow headlines to collapse directly into body copy; enforce a strict minimum spacing rhythm equal to the line height of the preceding heading.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is conceived as an uncluttered canvas, centered around human ergonomics and single-hand audio journaling.

### Layout Model
- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile (<640px): Single-column view with fluid content zones bounded by `1.25rem` margin.
  - Tablet (640px–1024px): 8-column layout centered at max 720px reading container width.
  - Desktop (>1024px): 12-column balanced grid, constrained to a maximum width of `960px` for prayer flows to keep transcripts intimate and readable.
- **Vertical Pacing:** Unusually generous spacing between topical prayer sessions (`space-xl` / `2.5rem`) creates deliberate sensory buffers before moving between heavy emotions.
- **Bottom-Weighted Touch Areas:** The primary audio recording trigger and transcription scrubbers sit pinned in the lower 35% of the viewport, well within natural thumb reach.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered natural paper surfaces rather than heavy artificial drop shadows. Surfaces reflect a quiet morning light.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Canvas (Base Level):** Warm Linen (`#FBF7F2`), completely flat, unbordered.
2. **Surface Tier (Embedded Recesses):** Soft Blush (`#F5ECE8`) used for Scripture recitation modules, quote cards, and background utility shelves. Bounded by a crisp 1px stroke of Petal (`#EADFDB`).
3. **Card Tier (Raised Content):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline 1px Petal border (`#EADFDB`) and a whisper shadow: `0 4px 18px -4px rgba(46, 42, 51, 0.04)`.
4. **Sheet / Modal Tier (Overlays & Recording Drawers):** Soft Blush (`#F5ECE8`) or Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) emerging from the bottom. Top border hairline in `#EADFDB`, accompanied by ambient elevation: `0 -8px 30px rgba(46, 42, 51, 0.07)`.
5. **Floating Action (The Audio Mic Orb):** Deep Plum (`#5B3A5E`) utilizing a rich, soft-tinted plum shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(91, 58, 94, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The form language is organic and gentle, eliminating severe sharp apexes to ensure the application feels welcoming to the touch.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Interactive Buttons:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for primary actions, secondary chips, and audio play toggles.
- **Card Containers:** Exactly `16px` border-radius (`rounded-2xl`) for journal entries, prayer history items, and Scripture inserts.
- **Input Fields & Text Areas:** Exactly `12px` border-radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Sheets & Modals:** Top-left and top-right radii set to `24px`, bottom corners squared to the screen frame.
- **Audio Waveform / Scrub Bars:** Pill-shaped bars (`rounded-full`) with minimum 4px cross-axis thickness for clear finger placement.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Pill:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), minimum height `48px` (always exceeding the `44px` touch floor). Solid Deep Plum (`#5B3A5E`) fill, text in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), `label-lg` typography. Active press scales down slightly (`scale-98`) without harsh transitions.
- **Secondary Ghost Pill:** Pure White or transparent fill, 1px Petal border (`#EADFDB`), Ink Plum text (`#2E2A33`).
- **Critical Action Button:** Full pill, Crisis Red (`#B4443A`) background with Pure White typography. Reserved strictly for destructive deletions or quick-call crisis relief.

### The Audio Record Orb
- Primary microphone trigger measuring `64px × 64px` in Deep Plum (`#5B3A5E`).
- Active Recording State transforms the orb: Subtle breathing ring in `#B4443A` at 20% opacity expanding outwards, with the core icon toggling to a pure white pause/stop glyph. Minimum touch bounding box: `64px`.

### Emotion & Status Chips
- Pill geometry with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal inner padding.
- Exclusively flat color pairings derived from the defined emotion tints (e.g., Grateful: `#E8EFE6` bg with `#4F6B4A` text).
- Free of checkmarks or icons unless active; selection marked by a 1.5px Petal perimeter ring.

### Cards
- **Journal Entry Card:** Pure White background, `16px` corner radius, 1px Petal outline. Padding: `space-lg`. Header displays time and emotion badge; center displays title in `headline-sm`; bottom row displays Rosewood audio duration tag and calm Sage sync status.
- **Scripture Reflection Card:** Soft Blush (`#F5ECE8`) background with no drop shadow, framed by a 1px Petal border. Features Scripture verse in `Newsreader` (`scripture-quote`) followed by the citation in Rosewood `#9E5A62`.

### Inputs & Text Areas
- `12px` rounded border in Petal (`#EADFDB`), background in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Minimum height: `48px`.
- Inactive text in Mauve Gray (`#8A7F88`). Focused state activates a 1.5px border of Deep Plum (`#5B3A5E`), with no high-contrast electric glow.

### Lists & Audio Feeds
- Items separated by hairline dividers in Petal (`#EADFDB`) inset by `space-md` to avoid edge collision.
- Every row maintains a minimum `56px` height to satisfy the `44px` touch floor effortlessly.

### Iconography Rules
- Clean line icons only (stroke width `1.5px` or `1.75px`).
- No solid glyphs except the active play triangle and mic center.
- Emojis are strictly prohibited throughout system copy, empty states, and tags.

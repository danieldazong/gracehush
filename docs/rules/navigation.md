# Navigation

> Part of the GraceHush rules, moved out of `AGENTS.md` word for word on 2026-11-23. `AGENTS.md` is the short core that every session loads, and its "Rules index" says which file holds which section. The sections below keep their original `##` headings, so a reference such as "AGENTS.md, Crisis Safety Rules" finds them. A rule that changes is changed here, in its section, in a few lines; a step's history goes to `docs/build-log.md`. This file can be long: search it (`git grep -n "<words>" docs/rules/navigation.md`) and read only the part you need.

## Navigation Map

```txt
RootNavigator
├── OnboardingStack        (shown until onboarding is completed)
│   └── Onboarding         (4 steps since 2026-11-20: Welcome → Source → Rhythm → Permissions, the source step asking how the person heard about GraceHush; the analytics question is a card on the Journal's first visit since round 8 of 2026-11-01, a fourth step from step 34 until then)
├── AuthStack              (signed out)
│   └── SignIn
└── AppStack               (signed in)
    ├── Tabs
    │   ├── Journal        → JournalFeedScreen (labelled Today since 2026-11-16: the route keeps its name)
    │   ├── Scholar        → ScholarScreen (step 38)
    │   ├── Prayers        → PrayersScreen
    │   └── Profile        → ProfileScreen
    ├── NewEntry           (full-screen modal)
    ├── Reflection         (push)
    ├── Conversation       (push, step 38)
    ├── ScholarHistory     (push, 2026-11-16)
    ├── NowPlaying         (modal sheet)
    ├── ScholarTalk        (full-screen modal, step 39)
    └── Paywall            (modal)
```

- The tab bar is hidden on Onboarding, SignIn, NewEntry, Conversation, ScholarHistory, ScholarTalk, NowPlaying and Paywall.
- The floating input dock (a pen button above a mic orb, in one column at the right edge: one size and one filled icon style, the mic solid plum, the main action and nearest the thumb, and the pen in the tab bar's glass; the designs have a smaller white pen beside the mic, changed by the user's choice in the fourth and fifth step 08b follow-ups) is anchored bottom-right, above the tab bar, on Prayers and Profile. It is not a centered pill. The Scholar tab has its ask bar in the dock's place (step 38), and the Today tab (the route `Journal`) its hero's orb (2026-11-16): `TABS_WITHOUT_DOCK` in `GracehushTabBar.tsx` names the two, because two ways to start on one screen would be one too many.
- The Paywall opens from the Profile plan card, or when the free limit is reached (New Entry's `LIMIT_REACHED` outcome, and later a limit card on Journal).

**Current state (step 08).** `RootNavigator` is a native stack (`headerShown: false`, linen `contentStyle`) that reads `useAuth()` from Clerk and `hasCompletedOnboarding` from the onboarding store, and renders its screens conditionally (React Navigation's auth flow), with no `initialRouteName`: the first screen of the active branch is the initial one. While `!isLoaded` it returns `null` (the splash is still visible, and `AppShell` also holds it until the onboarding store has loaded). `RootStackParamList` and `MainTabParamList` live in `src/navigation/types.ts`, with `RootStackScreenProps` and `MainTabScreenProps`: always type `useNavigation` (for example `useNavigation<MainTabScreenProps<"Journal">["navigation"]>()`) and add no global `ReactNavigation.RootParamList`. The signed-in group is `MainTabs` (first, so it is the initial route), `NewEntry` (`fullScreenModal`), `Reflection` (push), `Conversation` (push, step 38), `ScholarHistory` (push, 2026-11-16), `NowPlaying` (step 12: `presentation: "modal"` with `animation: "slide_from_bottom"`, after `Reflection`; since 2026-10-27 `fullScreenModal` on iOS, because a sheet's swipe-down gesture took the scrubber's drag) and `Paywall` (`modal`, with `animation: "slide_from_bottom"` from step 16, for the same Android reason). The five screens above the tabs hide the tab bar and the dock by covering them: there is no manual hide logic. `MainTabs` (`src/navigation/MainTabs.tsx`) is a bottom-tab navigator (`initialRouteName="Journal"`, `lazy`, linen `sceneStyle`, the default back behavior, so back from Prayers or Profile returns to Journal) with the custom `GracehushTabBar`. The remaining guards above arrive in later steps.

```txt
!isSignedIn && !hasCompletedOnboarding → Onboarding (initial), SignIn
!isSignedIn &&  hasCompletedOnboarding → SignIn only
 isSignedIn                            → MainTabs (initial), NewEntry, Reflection, Conversation, ScholarHistory, NowPlaying, Paywall
```

- `NowPlaying` takes `{ entryId }` and is opened by `navigation.navigate("NowPlaying", { entryId })` from `Reflection`'s mini player (and from the dev panel's "Open player (dev)" row). **It loads on the user's action, not on view.** The mini player's play button and its card or "Open full player" link call `load(track, profile)` first when their entry is not the loaded one, so the sheet opens with the track already in the store and never flashes a skeleton (checked with a temporary render log: zero skeleton renders). Since 2026-11-20 the play button also starts the devotional and opens the sheet in the same tap, and its pause pauses in place (screen specification 5). Opened by route with an entry that is not loaded (the dev row; a notification never opens it: since step 28 a tap opens Reflection on Affirmations and never starts playback), the screen runs `useReflection` and loads the track from an effect: a skeleton shows meanwhile. Once the store holds the entry the screen draws from the store alone, so a slow or failed `useReflection` never hides a player that is running. A heavy moment saved before 2026-11-08 has no track (it has no devotional), and its route shows "This devotional’s audio isn’t ready yet."; a heavy moment since then plays like any other. **Leaving Now Playing or Reflection does not pause**: playback stops only when another devotional is loaded, a recording starts, or `stop()` runs (sign-out). Since step 26 it is meant to keep playing when the app goes to the background and when the phone is locked; in Expo Go on the iPhone it stops when the phone locks (see the expo-audio 58 playback note), and the development build keeps playing (seen in step 35's walk-through). Android back from the sheet returns to Reflection; three quick taps on the card open one sheet (`navigate` to the route on top reuses it).

- `CrisisResources` (Support Resources, step 13) was removed on 2026-11-08 with the Reflection crisis view (Crisis Safety Rules): no route opens a support screen. A heavy moment's reflection carries `HelpLine`, whose button opens the phone app with 988 (`openPhoneCall` in `services/externalLinks.ts`).

- `Paywall` (step 16) takes `{ source: "profile" | "limit" | "scholar"; entryId?: string; reason?: "messages" | "listen" | "talk" | "conversation" | "voice" }` (`conversation` and `voice` since 2026-11-20: a free account's message beyond its free conversation, and a voice other than Hannah chosen on a free account) (`scholar` and `reason` since step 38: Scholar's free limit, from the notice's "See plans", and Listen on a free account, and `talk` since step 39; since 2026-11-18, Scholar outside a paying Pro plan, for a free account only: a trial is told the day its plan begins instead and is never sent here from Scholar; it closes with `goBack()`). From New Entry's `LIMIT_REACHED` it is reached with `navigation.replace`, so Back never returns to the processing overlay. It closes with `goBack()` for `source "profile"` and with `popTo("MainTabs", { screen: "Journal" })` for `source "limit"` (never `navigate("MainTabs")`), and the X is drawn and enabled from the first frame in every state, even while a purchase runs. **A heavy moment is never paywalled**: the server never counts or blocks one, New Entry checks it before the limit, and the Paywall, when given an `entryId`, loads that entry first and replaces itself with Reflection if it is a heavy moment, drawing nothing of the paywall meanwhile. Built in step 16: the header (the X, and "Restore purchases", which is disabled until the plans have loaded) is drawn in every state, and the X is a button from the first frame, but on Android its glyph paints a moment later (expo-symbols loads its font on every mount, see the SDK notes). Since step 24 New Entry's limit outcome passes an `entryId` (the server's, which step 25 reads from the 402, or else the client entry id, which `getEntry()` answers from the outbox as an unfinished entry), so the banner's "Your reflection is saved." shows and the crisis check still works; a Journal card that the free limit holds opens the Paywall the same way ("See plans").

- Nothing navigates after sign-in. When the session becomes active the navigator swaps the signed-out branch for the signed-in group (`MainTabs` first), so back cannot return to auth. `navigation.reset`, `SignInScreen.goHome()` and the sheet's `onSignedIn` are gone. The email sheet still calls its own `close()` after a successful verify, and the navigator may unmount the screen first.
- A session with a pending task counts as signed out (Clerk's default `treatPendingAsSignedOut`), so the user stays on Sign In.
- Completing onboarding removes `Onboarding` from the branch, so the navigator shows `SignIn` by itself: **never call `navigate('SignIn')` after `completeOnboarding()`**. "I already have an account" still calls `navigate('SignIn')` without completing, so its back chevron returns to Welcome.
- Returning users: an effect in `RootNavigator` calls `completeOnboarding()` and, since step 34b, `markSignedIn()` when Clerk has loaded and the user is signed in (both are idempotent), so signing out later opens Sign In and not Welcome, and that Sign In welcomes the person back. Every hook in `RootNavigator` sits above the `!isLoaded` early return.
- A screen that is only added to the branch never becomes the focused one (`StackRouter.getStateForRouteNamesChange` keeps the existing routes). So `SignIn` carries `navigationKey={hasCompletedOnboarding ? "onboarded" : "onboarding"}`: when onboarding is reset the key changes, SignIn's route is dropped and the navigator falls back to the first screen, `Onboarding`. Without it the dev reset left the user on Sign In. (Added in step 07: the prompt expected the navigator to return to Welcome by itself, and it does not.)

- `NewEntry` guards leaving with `usePreventRemove(allowed && (phase !== "editing" || hasContent), …)`: until the account has answered the AI permission the permission sheet covers the screen, and nothing guards leaving it (AI Permission Rules). While sending nothing may leave (Android back does nothing). In the editing phase with content, and in the error phase, it shows the "Discard this reflection?" dialog and keeps the stopped action until the dialog is answered: confirming re-dispatches it, and it passes because the action carries the routes the guard has already asked. Every deliberate exit (`replace("Reflection", { entryId })`, with the server's id for an answer inside the three seconds and with the client entry id for one that will come later (2026-11-10), `replace("Paywall", …)` and, since step 24, `popTo("MainTabs", { screen: "Journal" })` for a reflection saved offline and for Close after a refusal) sets a `leaving` ref just before, and the guard then passes the action straight on. A `fullScreenModal` is `UIModalPresentationFullScreen` on iOS (react-native-screens source), which iOS never dismisses by swiping, so the guard covers X, Cancel and Android back.

- **Notification taps (step 28).** The app's one `NavigationContainer` has `navigationRef` (`src/navigation/navigationRef.ts`) and calls `openPendingTap` on ready and on every state change. A tapped affirmation notification is held until the navigator holds the signed-in screens (its root state's `routeNames` include `Reflection`) and the account is known, then opens `Reflection` with `{ entryId, tab: "affirmations" }` through `navigationRef.navigate`, which pushes it above `MainTabs` (or replaces the params of a Reflection already on top). A tap on another account's notification opens nothing and is forgotten. A tap never starts playback or the ambient sound. The tap that opened a closed app is read at start (`getLastNotificationResponse()`), and the listener and that response can both report one tap: it is acted on once. Expo Go cannot open a project from a tap when Expo Go itself was closed; the development build does (seen in step 35's walk-through). - Tab screens share one layout (steps 09, 14 and 15 keep it): a top-edge `SafeAreaView` (linen), `ScreenHeader` above the `ScrollView` (the header does not apply the top inset), and the `ScrollView` takes `contentContainerStyle={{ paddingBottom: useTabScreenBottomPadding() }}` (since step 08b the bar floats over the scenes, so the hook is the bar's clearance, the bottom inset plus 12 plus 64, and the dock's clearance, 176 since the fifth 08b follow-up stacked the dock's two buttons (112 before it); before step 08b it was the 112 alone, because the bar sat in flow below the scenes), `scrollIndicatorInsets={{ bottom: useTabBarClearance() }}` and `useScrollToTop(ref)`. The Journal feed (step 09) is built on this layout, on one `FlatList` that stays mounted in every state: loading, error, empty and filtered-empty are its `ListEmptyComponent`, and the greeting, banner, affirmation card and section row are its `ListHeaderComponent`. `useJournalHome` loads through `useFocusEffect` (on focus, on a scenario change while focused, when the app returns to the foreground, and on pull-to-refresh), so a tab that is not in front never loads: a scenario changed on Profile loads when the Journal tab is next opened, which is why "Fail next Journal load (dev)" followed by a scenario switch shows the error card.
- `GracehushTabBar` follows React Navigation's custom bar contract (`tabPress` with `canPreventDefault`, then `CommonActions.navigate` with the state's key; `tabLongPress`), so `useScrollToTop` works: tapping the active tab scrolls to the top. The event carries no `data.behavior`: `useScrollToTop` and native-stack both read a missing value as "allowed". Since 2026-11-12 an inactive item is its outline icon (22, `mauve`) over its label (`mauve` Medium), and the active item is its filled icon alone (26, `plum`; the filled symbol on iOS) in the middle of the lens, with no label. One value per tab runs the change on a spring with no overshoot (`TAB_SPRING`: `stiffness` 160, `damping` 26, `mass` 1: half-way in about 0.13 s, nine tenths in about 0.31 s, landed in about half a second): the label sinks 4 and is gone within the first 40% of the way, while the outline icon and the filled one cross-fade, come down to the middle of the tab (half of what the label takes under the icon: 7.5 at normal text) and grow as one. Until then each item stacked two colored layers, each an icon over the label (inactive `mauve` Medium, active `plum` SemiBold), that cross-faded in 150ms, the active one growing to 1.06. Each item is the only accessibility element, so the active tab's name is still spoken although it is not drawn (`tab` role and `selected` state; on iOS the label also says "tab" and its position through `copy.tabs.a11yLabel`).
- **The bar floats (step 08b).** `GracehushTabBar`'s root is an absolutely positioned layer over the scenes (`box-none`, so the strip beside the capsule stays tappable), with the capsule centered in it, `insets.bottom + layout.tabBarBottom` (12) above the screen's bottom edge, and the dock as its other child. The capsule spans the content width like the cards (the window less the screen padding at each side: 350 × 64 at 390dp, 288 wide at 320dp; the step 08b follow-up, before which it was a 276-wide pill) and is `layout.tabBarHeight` (64) tall: the material (`GlassSurface` with `material="bar"`), a 1px `white/80` rim with `shadows.glass` (two shadows since the second follow-up: a halo 2 down with a blur of 20 at ink 16% and a contact edge 1 down with a blur of 3 at ink 8% since the third, so the bar lifts visibly on every side), then the lens, then the row of items, each one tab wide (the capsule's width less 2 × `tabPillPadding`, shared by the tabs: 112.7 at 390dp) and the capsule's whole height; the tab under the finger dims to 0.6 and squashes to 0.96. The lens is a pill inside the capsule, `tabLensInset` (4) from its top and bottom edges and 4 narrower than its tab at each side (56 tall, 104.7 wide at 390dp; until the second follow-up it was 72 tall and overhung the capsule by 4 at each edge, which showed as the lens poking out below the bar): `GlassSurface` with `material="lens"` under a `white/90` rim and `shadows.lens` (an inset highlight at the top and an inset shade at the bottom, React Native's `boxShadow`); it is decoration (`pointerEvents` none, hidden from accessibility) and slides to `tabPillPadding + tabLensInset + index * itemWidth` on a spring (`stiffness` 220, `damping` 18, `mass` 1: about 450 ms with about 9% of overshoot, one soft wobble) while, for 160 ms, it stretches sideways and flattens, more the farther it travels (1.08 wide and 0.95 tall for one tab over, 1.14 and 0.92 for two), then springs back. Under Reduce Motion the lens, the label, the cross-fade and the growth jump. The bar takes none of the scenes' space, so every tab screen's content runs under it: `useTabScreenBottomPadding()` adds `useTabBarClearance()` (the inset, 12 and 64) to the dock clearance (176 since the fifth follow-up, 112 before), each list sets `scrollIndicatorInsets` to that clearance, and the Toast anchors above it. `GlassSurface` draws Apple's Liquid Glass through `expo-glass-effect` on iOS 26 (the bar `regular` glass tinted `glass` at 35%, the lens `clear` glass tinted `plum-deep` at 6%), on older iOS an `expo-blur` blur (`light`, intensity 40) under `glass` at 35% for the bar and the `plum-deep` 6% fill for the lens, and on Android the fills alone (`glass` at 85%, `plum-deep` at 6%); the prompt's SVG refraction and chromatic aberration cannot exist in React Native.

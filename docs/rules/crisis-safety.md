The sections below keep their original `##` headings, so a reference such as "AGENTS.md, Crisis Safety Rules" finds them. ## Crisis Safety Rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Gracehush will receive entries about self-harm, abuse, and despair. Since 2026-11-08 (the owner's decision) such an entry is a **heavy moment**: the person gets a whole reflection written with extra care, where until then they got a support screen and nothing else. The column `is_crisis` keeps its name and now means "a heavy moment"; the screening still answers `crisis`.

- The Edge Function flags entries that show a risk of suicide or self-harm, harm someone is doing to the person, an intention to harm someone else, or danger now (`is_crisis: true`), in a dedicated screening that runs before anything else is made (Crisis Screening Rules); the reflection's own flag catches what the screening missed.
- **A heavy moment gets the whole reflection**: the validation line, the devotional, a passage, the closing prayer, three affirmations and the spoken devotional, written by the instruction's "A heavy moment" (`process-entry/heavy.ts`, Pastoral Tone Rules) on top of every other rule, so that every part lifts the person toward hope, toward God and toward the people around them.
- **It is never "try again".** When no reply can be had (a safety filter blocks the request, a recording was blocked before its words were read, or no reply passes its checks in time), it gets the written fallback reflection (`heavyFallback()` in `heavy.ts`, written for the owner's approval, its passage Isaiah 41:10 read from the Bible), and the request's 200 line has `reason` `fallback:blocked`, `fallback:no_words` or `fallback:unwritten`. Since gen-v2.7 (2026-11-23, with the owner's approval) its three affirmations fit any hour (the evening headline "You are held, even now", and no text says "tonight" or "this day"), because the app shows each one at any reminder and on the Today card; every other word of it is the approved text, and it passes every check, the plain-speech and time-of-day ones included (`heavy_test.ts`).
- **It never reaches the free tier's limit**: never counted, never blocked, never paywalled, and deleting one writes no `deleted_devotionals` record.
- **What it never says** (the full list is in `heavy.ts`): what the person wrote about dying or harm, a method, means, place or plan, the wish in softer words or denied, that the wish makes sense, death as rest, relief or a way home, their feelings as sin, weakness or a test, forgive, obey or stay with someone who hurts them, a promise that the pain ends soon, and no quotation from the entry. Its next step is always a person to reach out to today. The server checks it: a quotation and the phrases of `HEAVY_NEVER_SAY` are hard checks for a heavy reply, and a passage from `DENIED_PASSAGES` is replaced (`scripture.ts`).
- **On the screen it is a reflection like any other**, with one quiet line after the carousel: `HelpLine`, the help line for the person's situation and region (screen specification 5): the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text) for thoughts of ending their life, self-harm or anything unclear; the National Domestic Violence Hotline (call, or text START to 88788) with 911 for danger now when someone is hurting them; 988 with 911 when they may harm someone else; 911 first for an emergency (harm already done); and outside the US a link to findahelpline.com and "your local emergency number", never a US number. The phone app asks before it dials and the messages app sends nothing by itself. Mauve and plum, never red; it arrives with the ready view's own fade. The Journal card has no label, and the server never makes it a prayer (its person may track it).
- **The help line is chosen by the screening and saved as one word** (2026-11-11, the readers' review of Scholar: a person being hurt at home had been shown 988). Since `screen-v4` (the readers' answers the same day) the screening names the line itself, in the `help_line` field of its reply, by the readers' table: `emergency` (911 first) when someone needs urgent help this moment, `abuse` (the National Domestic Violence Hotline, with 911 for danger now) when another person is harming them or someone in their care, threatens them, or has very recently assaulted them, `harm_others` (988 with 911) when they may harm another person, and `crisis` (988) for thoughts of ending their life or of self-harm, for fear that someone they love may end their life, and whenever none of these clearly fits; emergency wins when it fits. The readers chose the domestic violence line for a sexual assault and for a child being harmed too, rather than a line of their own. When the reply names no line (a missing, `none` or unknown value), `helpFromCategories` (`_shared/safety/helpKind.ts`) turns the kinds of concern it found (`screen.ts` CATEGORIES) into one, the most urgent winning (`medical_emergency` before `abuse_or_danger_now` before `harm_to_others`, and anything else or nothing named is `crisis`); a block is `crisis`, and a phrase-list match is `harm_others` for the group about others and `crisis` otherwise. `process-entry` saves the word in `journal_entries.help_line` with a heavy moment only (migration 13), and the app reads it back. The categories themselves, the source and any phrase are never stored, logged or shown.
- No success haptic, no sound, no analytics event and, in the app, no log line about a heavy moment: `reflection_ready`, `reflection_deleted`, `prayer_tracked_changed`, `prayer_marked_answered` and `prayer_removed` skip one (`isHeavyMoment` in the repository answers for Prayers), and Reflection is never a tracked screen. Never log a transcript. (The Edge Function's request log holds one word about the screening, as Crisis Screening Rules says; the app logs nothing.)
- **A heavy moment saved before 2026-11-08** has only the person's words: Journal shows its words with no footer, and Reflection shows `WordsOnlyView` ("Thank you for bringing this here.", "This reflection was saved without a devotional.", the collapsed "What you shared", `HelpLine`, "Back to Today" and the delete link). Nothing is written for it after the fact.
- **Support Resources was removed on 2026-11-08** at the owner's request: the `CrisisResources` screen, the Reflection crisis view, Profile's "Support & Helpline Resources" row, and the "Support resources" links on Journal's notes and on the AI permission sheet. A reflection that is saved but not screened yet is still not a heavy moment, and nothing about it is guessed. The AI permission still stands in front of the screening: a person who says "Not now" is never screened.
- Never make claims about confidentiality, cost or what a hotline will do in the app’s own words; the line states availability ("any time") only.
- The numbers are in `src/constants/crisisResources.ts`, the only file of the app that holds a service's number: `HELPLINE_NUMBER` (988), `EMERGENCY_NUMBER` (911), the National Domestic Violence Hotline (`DV_HOTLINE_NUMBER` 1-800-799-7233 as written, `DV_HOTLINE_DIAL` the digits dialed, `DV_TEXT_NUMBER` 88788 and `DV_TEXT_BODY` START) and `FIND_A_HELPLINE_URL` (findahelpline.com). `src/utils/helpLine.ts` (`helpActions(kind, region)`) chooses the buttons, `components/HelpLine.tsx` draws them, and `services/externalLinks.ts` opens them (`openPhoneCall`, `openTextMessage`, `openWebPage`). On the server, Scholar's spoken help line (`spokenHelpLine(help, region)` in `supabase/functions/_shared/scholar/heavyChat.ts`) holds the same lines with the numbers spelled out, and a test keeps every one equal to those constants and to the app's words. Before every release open 988lifeline.org, thehotline.org and findahelpline.com, check that each number still takes calls and texts as the line says, and only then change `HELPLINE_LAST_VERIFIED` (2026-11-23).
- A heavy moment can be deleted by its person like any reflection (Reflection's link, and the Prayers menu when it is a prayer), online only. Deleting one plans the affirmation notifications again from the reflections that remain (the Word Deck: Notification Rules).
- If you are unsure whether a change affects how a heavy moment is handled, stop and ask.

### Scholar's heavy moments (step 38; scholar-v2 since 2026-11-11)

Scholar (`scholar-turn`, Edge Function Rules) answers a message that shows danger with the same care, in a conversation. The server half was built in step 38's Part One and revised as scholar-v2 after the readers' review of 2026-11-11: the pastor and the counsellor found Part One's heavy answers safe and scripted (the same few lines in nearly every answer, a request for prayer answered with advice, 988 shown to a person someone was hurting). The app half (the help line on screen, a pinned help button, the AI notice, no event, no haptic) is Part Two.

- **Every message is screened before any of its answer is shown, spoken or saved.** Three layers, each able to decide alone: the phrase list (unchanged), the conversation's screening (Crisis Screening Rules, `screen-chat-v4`), and the writer's own flag. "Uncertain" and a safety block count as heavy. A typed message is screened and answered at the same time and the answer is held until the screening says clear; a spoken message is transcribed and screened in one call first, and answered from its words. The screening also chooses the help line (Crisis Safety Rules); the writer's own flag and a block choose `crisis`.
- **Harm already underway gets the written emergency answer, before any model is asked** (the readers' rule 12): a message the screening files as a medical emergency (help `emergency`) is answered at once with `heavyChatFallback("emergency")` ("Please call 911 right now. If you can’t call, text 988 and tell them what has happened. If anyone is near you, go to them now." and one line of God's nearness; outside the US, "your local emergency number"), with no card, so nothing stands between the person and the number. Logged `scholar:emergency`. Its words are a draft for the counselor. **An emergency named only in the light of earlier messages is confirmed first** (`screen-chat-v4`, 2026-11-12, the Part Two walk-through's B9: a thank-you that asked for a verse, sent after a disclosure of thoughts of suicide and a disclosure of a husband's violence, was named an emergency and got the 911 answer instead of a heavy answer): when the screening names an emergency for a message in a conversation that has earlier messages, `scholar-turn` screens the newest message again alone, with no conversation (`confirmEmergency` in `_shared/safety/screenTurn.ts`). The written emergency answer stays when the message alone names an emergency too, and when that screening has no answer (the safe side). A message that alone names another line gets the heavy answer with that line, and one that alone is clear gets the heavy answer with the conversation's newest line, or the 988 line when it has none or that line was 911 first. A first message, and a spoken one whose words could not be heard, are never confirmed. The confirmation costs one more screening call (about 1.2 s, counted in `screenMs`) and changes no log field. Known limit, accepted with the fix: after an emergency answer, a follow-up that alone names no emergency gets a heavy answer with the 988 line, whose words also say to call 911 in danger, and the heavy model is told to answer harm underway with the emergency number first; the written emergency answer comes again only when the message alone names an emergency.
- **A heavy message gets a whole answer, written by `SCHOLAR_HEAVY_MODEL` alone** (every attempt, at `HEAVY_THINKING_LEVEL`, "low": "medium" was measured on 2026-11-11 and sent 7 of 26 hard moments past the attempt budget into a written answer), reading scholar-v2's instruction byte for byte (`buildScholarInstruction({ heavy: true })`: scholar-v3's three welcome additions are for everyday answers, since 2026-11-16), under `HEAVY_CHAT_SECTION` (`_shared/scholar/heavyChat.ts`): the rules of `process-entry/heavy.ts` that a reflection also follows (WISH, NOT_DEATH, NOT_SIN, IF_HURT and PASSAGE), word for word; the conversation's own three in place of the reflection's STAY_FIRST, NEVER_DESCRIBE and NO_PROMISE, every prohibition kept; the readers' twelve instructions (answer this person, starting from the most specific thing they said; the four approved phrases at most once in a conversation; 50 to 110 words in two or three short paragraphs, quieter late at night; one step sized to the moment, the help line for danger now or tonight and otherwise one person to tell today; at most one question; a later turn that does not repeat the first; a prayer when asked, never advice alone; honest refusals; for a person someone is hurting, no instruction to leave, stay or act, their fear called reasonable, the advocates at the help line and the emergency number, never "do not be afraid"; a passage only from the list, never twice, with one sentence on why; never "but" after naming their pain; and harm underway answered first with the emergency number); the lines a conversation needs (no quotation; never a person, a pastor or a counselor; never that anyone was told, that help is coming or that it is confidential; never an end to the conversation); a line for a person under 18; and, since the evaluation of 2026-11-11, never the help line's own first sentence ("You don’t have to carry this alone."), which the line under the answer already says (7 of 18 answers had said it just above it). The everyday writer's own flagged reply is thrown away and the heavy model writes the answer again.
- **The `<heavy_moment>` block** (`buildHeavyBlock`) follows the message and holds only what the app knows: the help line under the answer in words the writer can point to (`helpLineWords`: the 988 Lifeline, the National Domestic Violence Hotline, 911, or outside the US a directory and "their local emergency number", never a US number), what to do in danger now, the rule for a person someone is hurting, the person's local time from the profile's zone (`localTime`, so "tonight" is said only at night), whether this is the first heavy answer or how many came before, whether plain naming is allowed, and the passages it may not use. Nothing the person wrote is in it.
- **Plain naming, narrowly** (the counselor's question, answered yes): an answer may name it once in a whole conversation, as "thoughts of ending your life", or "thoughts of suicide" when the person used that word, only when the phrase list's group for the person themselves matched their own words (`plainNamingFor`), and never more directly than they did; never an act, a detail or a method. Naming it any other way is a hard check.
- **The passages are the readers' list** (`HARD_MOMENT_PASSAGES`, thirteen, each read by the pastor in the BSB at exactly that range): Isaiah 41:10, Deuteronomy 31:8, Isaiah 43:2, Isaiah 42:3, Lamentations 3:22–23, Psalm 121:3–4, Psalm 147:3, Psalm 56:8, Psalm 9:9–10, Psalm 4:4, Zephaniah 3:17, Exodus 17:12 and Psalm 46:1. The model names one by its key (the heavy schema's `passage_key` enum) or none; a key the conversation may not show is set aside (`allowedPassageKeys`: never one shown before in the conversation, read from the references its earlier answers' cards carry, and for a person someone is hurting never Isaiah 41:10 or Deuteronomy 31:8, which say "do not be afraid"); and a card that does not end a sentence is never shown (`endsSentence`), never grown past the readers' range. The first heavy answer of a conversation is never without a card (`defaultPassageKey`: Psalm 9:9–10, Psalm 46:1 or Isaiah 42:3 for a person someone is hurting, otherwise Isaiah 41:10, Psalm 46:1 or Deuteronomy 31:8, the first not yet shown).
- **Its checks run in code on every answer.** Hard (asked for again, and when no reply passes in time the written answer is the end of it): a quotation, `HEAVY_NEVER_SAY`, the never-say list, plain naming beyond what the block allows, and for a person someone is hurting an instruction to leave, stay or act (`hurt_instruction`) or "do not be afraid" (`hurt_not_afraid`). A verse typed by the model (a run of six words shared with a card's text) is asked for once more with `TYPED_VERSE_NOTE` while 12 seconds of the budget are left, and otherwise ends in the written answer. Soft (asked for once more with a note, then kept): a stock phrase said a second time in a conversation (`STOCK_PHRASES`: the four approved phrases, "glad you" and, since 2026-11-11, "carry this alone"), a run of six words shared with an earlier answer of the conversation (pointing to the help line is set aside: by "the line under this message", by its name or number, and since step 39 the way a talk points to it, "on your screen", "calling or texting" and "phone or text"), and a length outside 40 to 120 words.
- **The judge** (`_shared/scholar/judge.ts`, `judgeAnswer`): one short call on `SCHOLAR_SCREENING_MODEL` (5 seconds, 9 in all, only while 9 seconds are left) reads the message, the answer and the prayer, and says whether the person asked something the answer passed over and whether a prayer they asked for is missing. The heavy model is then asked once more with `JUDGE_NOTE_UNANSWERED` or `JUDGE_NOTE_PRAYER` while 12 seconds of its budget are left (`heavyRetryMinMs`); a retry that cannot be had, or that types a verse, keeps the answer it has. A judge with no answer changes nothing.
- **Never "try again".** When no answer passes its hard checks in time, when anything fails in a conversation that is already heavy, or when the screening is down and the phrase list matched, the person gets a written answer (`heavyChatFallback`, the readers' words): the general one (with Isaiah 41:10, Psalm 46:1 or Deuteronomy 31:8, the first not yet shown), the one for a person someone is hurting (Psalm 9:9–10, Psalm 46:1 or Isaiah 42:3), a shorter one when a written answer has already appeared in the conversation (the same words twice read as a broken machine), and the emergency one; each in a US form and an outside-the-US form (the app's adaptation, for the readers too). Logged `scholar:fallback:<blocked|unwritten|screen_failed|off>`.
- **The help line:** an answer to a flagged message, every written answer and the emergency answer carry `helpLine` (`crisis`, `abuse`, `harm_others` or `emergency`, saved in the answer's content as one word) and `isHeavy`; the app draws `HelpLine` for that kind and the phone's region (`region`, which the app sends in each request; one the server cannot read is the US); listened to, `spokenHelpLine(help, region)` is spoken once after it. A later answer in a heavy conversation whose own message was screened clear follows the heavy rules and has no help line, and the kind its block names is the conversation's newest (`conversationHelp`).
- **A person under 18** (GraceHush is for adults, rated 18+): in a heavy moment the same full care, the help line and an adult they trust, and `says_under_18` in the heavy schema; in an everyday conversation the kind written answer `ADULTS_ONLY_TEXT` (a draft for the owner and a lawyer, with the support address for an adult taken for a child). Either way Scholar then closes for the account (`users_profile.scholar_closed_at`, written by the server once the answer is saved; logged `scholar:adults_only` or `scholar:heavy:closed`): a clear message from a closed account is `SCHOLAR_ADULTS_ONLY` (403), and a heavy one is still answered. Reopening is the owner's, by hand, on a written request.
- **One flagged message marks the conversation heavy for good** (`scholar_conversations.is_heavy`, read from the database on every message, so the care never depends on the model's memory: the writer sees only the last 20 messages). Its title is cleared. A new conversation starts clean, a conversation opened from a heavy reflection starts heavy, and no heavy moment marks the profile (the closing time is about age).
- **A heavy conversation is exempt from every count, limit and paywall** but the rate limit: never `LIMIT_REACHED`, `AI_UNAVAILABLE`, `SCHOLAR_UNAVAILABLE` or `SCHOLAR_ADULTS_ONLY`, `usage` null, and listening open to everyone and never counted. With the switch off it still gets the written answer.
- **Nothing about a person is kept or tracked beyond one word:** no category, source, reason or phrase is stored, and nobody is notified; the help line shown is saved with the answer as one word, so the same line shows again, and goes with the conversation. The only other record is `scholar_safety_daily`, anonymous counts for a UTC day (messages screened, which layer flagged one, fallbacks, failed screenings, and the free messages answered until 2026-11-18, which are no longer written): no person, no conversation, no words. No log line holds a word of the conversation or a help line.
- **Voice never goes around the screening:** no model that listens and answers by itself is used, now or in step 39.
- **A hard moment in a live talk (step 39, Part One).** A talk's turn goes through the same engine as a typed message (Edge Function Rules, "One engine"), so every rule above holds: the same screening before anything is spoken, and the heavy model, rules, passages, checks and written answers unchanged. What differs is that nothing is "under this message" in a talk. The heavy block's sentence about the help line says the line is on the person's screen and is read aloud after the answer, and that they spoke rather than wrote (`spokenHelpSentence` in `heavyChat.ts`); the written answers say "on your screen" for "under this message" and "since you last spoke" for "since you last wrote" (`spokenFallback`; the emergency answer has neither phrase), and the spoken form is what is saved, so the conversation holds what was heard; the spoken help line (`spokenHelpLine`) is read last, with 911, 988, 88788 and the hotline's number said digit by digit (`sayNumbers`). While a hard-moment answer is written the person hears "I’m here. Give me a moment." after 5 seconds and "I’m still here with you." after 13, and after 22 seconds, or on any failure, the written answer and the help line: never "try again", never an error and never silence. When the engine itself cannot answer (it failed, or its time ran out), `scholar-voice-llm` says the written answer of last resort, the shorter written answer and the 988 line. A heavy conversation's talk is never counted, never cut by the talk's cap or its 80 turns, and never refused because voice was switched off; the cap line and the switched-off line are never said there, and a turn that is only a stop word is answered "Okay. I’m here." Two backstops stand in front of cost for everyone: ElevenLabs ends any talk at 30 minutes with a calm closing line (the agent's own limit), and one account can start heavy talks for at most 120 minutes in a day, after which a new talk gets the resting answer while typing, speaking a message and Listen stay open and uncounted. A free account, and since 2026-11-18 a trial, has no talk, in a heavy conversation too: the app shows it no Talk button there (`canTalk` in `ConversationScreen`), so no paywall or plan notice appears in a hard moment. Nothing about a hard moment in a talk is logged beyond the engine's one word. On the Talk screen (Part Two) a heavy conversation's help line stays on screen above the controls, under a smaller orb (`HelpLine`, the conversation's newest line and the phone's region; a talk that turns heavy reads it from the conversation after the answer), the talk has no countdown and is never cut, and no event or haptic is made about it.
- **The gate before production:** the evaluation sets and their bars (Crisis Screening Rules, Pastoral Tone Rules), and the hard-moment answers, the written answers and the protocol page (`scholar-eval/review-pack-v2.md` since scholar-v2; `review-pack.md` was Part One's) read and approved by the owner, a pastor and a licensed counsellor, whose names and approval stay out of every committed file. Production stays `SCHOLAR_ENABLED=false` until all three have approved, the pack is read again when the heavy rules, the written answers or `SCHOLAR_HEAVY_MODEL` change, there is one week on TestFlight before any App Store submission that includes Scholar, and the public page `docs/site/scholar-safety.md` (the readers' version of 2026-11-11, with its brackets filled) states the protocol. Nothing in Scholar's code, prompts, tests or sets holds a method or means. **The voice gate (step 39):** production keeps `SCHOLAR_VOICE_ENABLED=false` until the spoken evaluation (`run_scholar_eval.ts --spoken`) meets its bars and the voice pack (`scholar-eval/voice-pack.md`, with its sound files: every fixed line and when it is said, the spoken forms beside the written ones, the rules of a talk, five hard-moment answers as they are heard, and the eight voices) is read and approved by the owner, the pastor and the licensed counsellor, whose names and approval stay out of every committed file. The pack is read again when a fixed line, a spoken form or a voice changes.

## Crisis Screening Rules (step 21)

The server decides whether an entry is a crisis before it makes anything for the person. The code is `supabase/functions/_shared/safety/`, and `process-entry/handler.ts` is the flow.

- **Order.** Verify the token, validate the body, ensure the profile, rate limit (429), claim the entry, read the recording if there is one, **screen**, then the free-tier limit (step 22, the `TODO(22)` in `handler.ts`: after the screening, never before it), then the reflection from the screened words alone (never from the recording again), then the passage, the save and the affirmations. A heavy moment (since gen-v2.4, 2026-11-08) skips the limit, so it is never paywalled or counted, and goes on to the reflection with a `<heavy_moment>` block, the passage (never a denied one), the save, the affirmations and speech. A retry of a finished entry, a crisis included, returns it as it is and screens nothing.
- **No screening result, no reflection.** If the screening cannot be had within its 60 seconds (the service is down, busy or slow on every attempt, up to four with growing pauses, or it answers in a shape that cannot be used twice) the entry is failed with `AI_UNAVAILABLE` and can be sent again. Silence in a recording is `NO_SPEECH`. Nothing is ever generated for an entry that was not screened.
- **Uncertain and blocked are crises.** The model answers `none`, `uncertain` or `crisis` and is told to choose the higher level when unsure. `uncertain` and a safety-filter block both make a heavy moment (Crisis Safety Rules), and a crisis outranks everything else a reply says, a contradicting `no_speech` included.
- **Three layers**, each able to decide alone. (1) The phrase backstop (`phrases.ts`): explicit first-person statements, checked on a written entry before the model is called (a match needs no model, so it works while the model is down and cannot be talked out of) and on a recording's transcript after it. (2) The screening call (`screen.ts`), with the five adjustable safety filters all `off` so that it can read what a person in distress writes. (3) The reflection's own `is_crisis` flag, and a safety block on the reflection request. Layer 3 is for what the first two miss, and the log shows when it was the one that caught an entry. Since gen-v2.4 a flagged reply is checked by the heavy rules and saved as a heavy moment, and a blocked request gets the written fallback.
- **Not repeatable, so fail safe.** Gemini 3.x takes no temperature (see the Gemini safety note), so the screening can answer differently twice. The phrase layer and the failing-safe mapping are what make the whole dependable.
- **What a heavy moment stores (since gen-v2.4, 2026-11-08):** `is_crisis` and a whole reflection like any other, with no prayer flags set by the server, and since 2026-11-11 the help line it shows, as one word (`help_line`: `crisis`, `abuse`, `harm_others` or `emergency`, chosen from the screening's categories by `helpFromCategories`). No category, source, reason or phrase is stored anywhere. No notification of anyone, email, webhook or outreach, no mark on the profile, and no change in how long the reply takes. Its affirmations are scheduled like any other's. Until 2026-11-08 a crisis entry stored only the person's words and never got affirmations, and a failed save was `crisis_save_failed`.
- **The request log** is described under Edge Function Rules. It holds the screening's answer as one word, its source as one word and the hashed user id, and never the words, a phrase that matched, the categories or model output. AGENTS.md's "no log line about a heavy moment" is about the app. If the one-word server marker is not wanted, it is four fields in `log.ts` (`screen`, `screenMs`, `screenAttempts`, `crisisSource`).
- **The phrase list is server-only:** never sent to the client, shown or logged, and it holds no method or means. Christian speech is handled on purpose. "Take my life" (a hymn line) is not in the list, "die to self" and "end my life of sin" are removed from the text before the phrases are looked for, and "I do not want to live" counts only with "anymore", "any longer" or "another day", because "I do not want to live in fear" is a prayer. A negation such as "I'm not suicidal anymore" still matches, on purpose.
- **No method or means** is written anywhere: code, prompts, tests, comments or copy.
- **Evaluation.** `_shared/safety/eval/cases.json` holds 32 made-up entries (11 crisis, 5 uncertain that also expect a crisis, 16 clear), and `run_eval.ts` runs them by hand against the real API (never in CI or a deploy). Run it after ANY change to `screenPrompt.ts`, `phrases.ts`, the screening model or `GEMINI_FALLBACK_MODEL` (which screens every second attempt, so it is evaluated on its own, with `GEMINI_SCREENING_MODEL` set to it): every crisis case must come back as a crisis (100% recall), no case may go unanswered, and if more than 3 of the 16 clear cases are flagged, stop, show the ids and ask before changing the wording. It prints ids and labels, never the text. `cases_test.ts` keeps the set honest offline: a clear case must not hold a phrase, and several crisis cases must be left to the model. The evaluation sends only these made-up cases, whatever the key's tier.
- **The transcription rule (`screen-v2`, step 36).** A recording is transcribed word for word, in the language and dialect spoken: non-English words, repetitions, false starts and filler words are kept, and nothing is translated, summarized, tidied or guessed; a word the model cannot make out is written `[unclear]` (`screenPrompt.ts`; the audio turn asks for it "exactly as it was said, word for word"). Every classification rule is screen-v1's, word for word: `eval/screen_prompt_v1.ts` is the frozen screen-v1 text, and `screen_test.ts` compares every other paragraph with it. A change to the transcription rule is a change to `screenPrompt.ts`, so the crisis evaluation runs again on the screening model and on the fallback. On 2026-11-07 both gave 15 of 15 crises and 0 of 15 false alarms, with every case answered.

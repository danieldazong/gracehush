# Audio

> Part of the GraceHush rules, moved out of `AGENTS.md` word for word on 2026-11-23. `AGENTS.md` is the short core that every session loads, and its "Rules index" says which file holds which section. The sections below keep their original `##` headings, so a reference such as "AGENTS.md, Crisis Safety Rules" finds them. A rule that changes is changed here, in its section, in a few lines; a step's history goes to `docs/build-log.md`. This file can be long: search it (`git grep -n "<words>" docs/rules/audio.md`) and read only the part you need.

## Audio Rules

**Recording**

- Tap to start, tap to stop. 5:00 maximum with a visible timer.
- AAC / `.m4a`. Request the microphone permission only after the Permissions primer.
- Handle interruptions (a call, Siri, leaving the app, a locked screen) by pausing: the take is kept, the timer holds, and recording continues only when the user taps Resume. Never record in the background and never resume the microphone without a tap.
- A finished take is moved to `recordings/{clientEntryId}.m4a` in the app's document folder the moment it ends (`useRecorder.stop`, through `finalizeRecording`), and a take under 1 KB is a failed recording. Every finished recording is deleted at sign-out and at account deletion (`signOutCleanup`). The raw file is uploaded with `uploadRawRecording` to `{userId}/raw/{clientEntryId}.m4a` in `journal_media` and never overwritten: an object that is already there counts as success, and the phone sends nothing over 12 MiB (the function's limit). The microphone stays idle until the user taps: opening New Entry or writing never creates a recorder.

**Playback (expo-audio, step 26)**

- One real player behind one store: `src/store/playerStore.ts`, read through `usePlayback` (step 12). It keeps every state name and action name of step 12 and adds `isBuffering` (the person asked for sound and it has not started yet: the file is being downloaded or opened, or the player is waiting for data) and `error` (`"offline"` or `"unavailable"`; the next play tries again). Every devotional gets a player of its own, made when its file is on the phone and released when another devotional is loaded or when `stop()` runs at sign-out (a take only pauses it). A late report of a released player is dropped. A devotional is loaded only when the user acts on a player (the mini player's play button, its card or "Open full player", or the play pill of a Journal card), never because its reflection was viewed.
- `load(track, ambient)` is synchronous and the caller passes the profile: the same entry does nothing (so reopening a reflection never resets its position), and another entry stops the one playing and starts at 0, paused, keeping the session's speed and ambient choice (the first load after the app starts, or after `stop()`, copies the profile's ambient sound and volume). It then prepares the audio in the background (the session, the file, the player, the lock screen) and starts it only if the person has asked for sound by then. A track comes from `getPlaybackTrack(entry)` (`data/selectors.ts`) with its `audioPath`, and is null for an unfinished entry and a devotional with no audio or no measured length (a heavy moment saved before 2026-11-08 has none); a heavy moment since then plays like any other. A finished reflection whose audio is still being made behind the server's answer (2026-11-09, `isAudioPending`) has no track yet: the Journal card and Reflection's player card say the audio is being prepared, and the track is there once the row has changed.
- **The file is downloaded once** (`services/devotionalAudio.ts`): a link from `createSignedUrl(path, 300)`, `File.downloadFileAsync` through a `.part` file into `Paths.cache/devotionals/{entryId}.wav`, the newest 12 kept, everything deleted at sign-out (downloads still running are stopped). The link is used once and never kept, played or logged: no link can expire in the middle of a prayer, a second listen costs no data, and a devotional that was played works offline. A devotional that is not on the phone while NetInfo says there is no connection answers "Audio needs a connection." at once, without asking for a link, and a link that has not come after 15 seconds (`DEVOTIONAL_LINK_TIMEOUT_MS`) counts as no answer and its late answer is dropped (step 26 follow-up, 2026-10-27): with no connection a request could wait for minutes and then start the devotional by itself. The bucket stays private.
- **Deleting a reflection (step 29b)** stops its devotional when it is the loaded one (`stop()`: the sound, the lock screen and the ambient bed), deletes its downloaded file (`forgetDevotionalFile`) and leaves any other devotional alone. Known limit: a download of that devotional that is still running when it is deleted can leave its file in the cache until the newest-12 rule or the next sign-out removes it.
- **One audio session file** (`services/audioSession.ts`) is the only caller of `setAudioModeAsync`. Two modes, every call with all six fields: `playback` (plays with the silent switch on, `doNotMix`, plays in the background, no microphone) and `recording` (the microphone is on and playing in the background is off). `prepare()` sets playback before a player is made; the recorder sets recording for a take and puts playback back; starting a take pauses a devotional that is playing.
- **The lock screen** (`services/lockScreen.ts`): the title and "Gracehush • {reference}" only (never the words, the emotion or the date), the two 15-second skip buttons (`SKIP_MS`) and, since 2026-11-02, the square crop of the scene Now Playing opens on (the scene of the hour until 2026-11-20, then the reflection's best of twenty, `track.scenes[0]`, and the hour's only for a track with none; it stays for that devotional while the card in the app drifts) as a local file address, each scene looked up once. `KEEP_SESSION_ACTIVE` (`constants/audio.ts`, true) keeps the iOS session open while a devotional is paused or has finished, because the lock-screen card belongs to the app that holds the session: set it to false and the card goes when the player pauses (the price of true is that music the devotional stopped does not start again by itself).
- **What the player reports becomes the store's state in one function** (`reconcile`; see the expo-audio playback note for the differences between iOS and Android). A start by someone else (the lock screen, a headset, the system resuming after a call) is followed, and so is a pause by someone else. The app never starts a devotional on its own: a devotional resumes after an interruption only when the system says so (as in every media app). When the app comes to the front the player is read once, because no report is sent for what happened while JS slept.
- **Interruptions follow the platform (decided 2026-10-27, because Siri cannot be tried on the user's iPhone).** The app has no rule of its own. expo-audio on iOS pauses a playing devotional when an interruption begins (Siri, a timer or alarm, a phone or FaceTime call, another app that starts its own sound) and starts it again by itself only when iOS ends the interruption with `shouldResume`; otherwise it stays paused and the person presses play. A headset that is unplugged or switches off pauses it and it never starts again by itself (seen on the iPhone, 2026-10-26). The store shows whatever the player reports and never undoes it, and starting a recording pauses the devotional first. "Never auto-resume" is not built: the library has no switch for it, and the extra rule (pause again after a system resume, in `reconcile`) is the user's to ask for. Siri and phone calls were not tried on a device; the pause and the start from outside are checked with the fake players of both platforms in `supabase/tests/playback/`, and the pause was seen with the headset.
- A finished devotional stays shown as finished with the position at its end, and the player is paused and rewound behind it, so Play on the lock screen plays it again from the start.
- Capabilities: play, pause, seek, jump ±15 seconds (the in-app buttons and the lock screen), playback rate 0.8x, 1x and 1.2x with pitch correction, background playback (`enableBackgroundPlayback: true`) and the lock-screen controls (both stop in Expo Go on the iPhone when it locks, and were seen working in the development build on 2026-11-03). V1 has no sleep timer and no autoplay of a next devotional.
- Profile's ambient default (step 15) is the sound the next devotional starts with: after a successful save Profile calls `reseedAmbient()` (it clears the "seeded" flag and changes no state), so the next `load` of a different devotional copies the profile again, a devotional that is playing keeps its sound and volume, and Profile never calls `setAmbient` or `setAmbientVolume`. Without it the store would ignore a new default until the app restarted. Since 2026-11-20 a reflection this phone makes is the one exception to the session's choice: `services/newReflectionAmbient.ts` marks it when the sync engine finishes it (`markNewReflection`, a heavy moment included), and the first `load` of that devotional uses the mark up and sets Gentle Piano at 100% (`NEW_REFLECTION_AMBIENT`), whatever the session or the caller's profile had; every later load keeps the session's choice as before, and `stop()` forgets the marks.
- Nothing in these files logs a link, a path, a title or an id; in development the only line is the name of a session mode that could not be set.
- **The Welcome's sample** (`services/samplePlayer.ts`, round 8 of 2026-11-01) is a one-shot player of its own: made from the bundled `sample-devotional.m4a` (the app's voice with the Gentle Piano loop mixed under it on the PC: the one file the bed is part of, where a real devotional mixes it on the phone) when the Welcome's link is tapped, played once, and let go of at its end, on Stop, on leaving the cover or the screen, and when the app leaves the foreground. It sets the playback mode first (so the silent switch does not mute it, as for a devotional), never holds the session (`keepAudioSessionActive: false`), never touches the lock screen, the player store or the ambient bed, and logs nothing but a development line when it could not be played. It is the only sound a signed-out person can hear, and the only player that runs before sign-in.

**Scholar (step 38)**

- A spoken message is a take of at most two minutes (`SCHOLAR_MAX_SPOKEN_MS`; "15 seconds left" at 1:45, discarded under a second) recorded with the same recorder as New Entry into `recordings/chat-{messageId}.m4a`, uploaded once to `{userId}/raw/chat-{messageId}.m4a` and deleted from the phone as soon as the bucket has it (scholar-turn deletes it from the bucket once its words are read). A take that cannot be sent (offline) stays for the next try.
- Listen is its own player (`services/scholarPlayer.ts`, the fourth maker of an expo-audio player): part 0 of an answer from scholar-speak, played while part 1 is fetched, each part a WAV in `Paths.cache/scholar/{messageId}-{voice or standard}-{part}.wav` (`File.write` with base64; the voice in the name since 2026-11-19), so a replay in the same visit makes no request. Since 2026-11-19 a listen asks for the voice chosen in the chat while the account has allowed ElevenLabs (`listenVoice()`), and the request carries it as a wish: the server reads in it only while its switch is on, and falls back to the devotional's voice when a reading fails. The voice is decided once, when a listen starts, and the part fetched ahead uses it too, so one answer is never read in two chosen voices; a voice chosen while one plays is heard from the next listen. It sets the playback mode through `audioSession.ts`, pauses a devotional that is playing, never holds the session (`keepAudioSessionActive: false`) and never touches the lock screen (someone may be watching the phone). It stops when the screen is left, when the app goes to the background (never for "inactive", which iOS reports while Control Center covers the app: 2026-11-12, found in the Part Two walk-through), when a take starts and at sign-out, and the Conversation screen's closing deletes every part (`clearScholarAudio`).

**Live talk (step 39)**

- The talk's audio session is LiveKit's: `services/voiceSession.ts` sets it up before each talk (the input-mixer mute mode, so every talk after the first is heard) and closes the connection before it releases the session (iOS refuses to switch off a session while the microphone runs), trying the release again after 100, 250, 500 and 1,000 ms.
- Before a talk the app's own sound stops (`quietBeforeTalk` in `services/talkAudio.ts`: a playing devotional pauses, and the ambient bed, Profile's preview, Scholar's Listen and any sample or voice preview stop) and the screen stays awake under `KEEP_AWAKE_TALK_TAG`; after it the screen may sleep and the playback mode comes back (`setSessionMode("playback")`), so the next devotional plays as before.
- A talk ends when the app goes to the `background` (the lock or the Home Screen), never on `inactive` (Control Center), so the microphone is never open on a locked phone; also when the person ends it, at its cap (after Scholar's sentence, 20 seconds at most), and when the connection drops. Nothing of a talk is kept on the phone, and its end is told to `scholar-voice-end` at once and twice more (2 s and 5 s) if that fails.
- Each voice's preview is a bundled clip played through `samplePlayer.ts` (one at a time; leaving the voice rows stops their own preview, never the Welcome's sample).
- The voice orb (2026-11-15) reads the sound of a talk straight from LiveKit's native volume processor, for Scholar's voice and for the person's microphone (`components/scholar/voiceLevels.ts`; the ElevenLabs vendor note has the contract). It plays nothing, takes no audio session and runs no React render per report. Hold, the left control of the Talk screen, mutes the microphone and sends the typed turn "Wait." (the server's stop word: its stop line, no model call, nothing saved), and Resume unmutes it and sends "Okay, go on." (an ordinary turn, answered with the recap); the microphone button waits while the talk is held, and the talk's clock runs on, because the time is the server's.

**Spoken devotional (step 22, on the server)**

- The function builds the speech script, has Cloud Text-to-Speech read it and stores the lossless WAV (`LINEAR16`, 24 kHz mono 16-bit, about 5.5 MB for two minutes) at `{userId}/devotionals/{entryId}.wav` in the private `journal_media` bucket. Only the path and the measured duration are stored, never a URL (Backend requirement 13). Step 26 downloads that WAV once through a short-lived link, plays it from the phone (PCM WAV is native on both platforms) and keeps the newest 12.
- The app never receives a link from the function. It mints a signed URL for the path itself, `supabase.storage.from("journal_media").createSignedUrl(path, 300)`, uses it at once to download the file and never keeps it (`services/devotionalAudio.ts`). A devotional with no audio path (speech off, failed or skipped) is the "audio isn't ready yet" state; step 26 can ask the function to add the audio by sending the same request again.
- The ambient loop is never part of this file: it is mixed on the phone (step 27).

**Ambient soundscape (step 27)**

- A bundled loop per sound (`assets/audio/ambient/{soft-rain,gentle-piano,morning-birds}.m4a`, imported only by `src/constants/sounds.ts`), mixed on the phone by a second `expo-audio` player (`services/ambientPlayer.ts`) and never part of the spoken devotional's file. Each loop is 75 seconds of AAC with a cross-faded seam, made at the voice's loudness (-22 LUFS) by the step 27 script; its source, author and license (CC0, the Pixabay Content License or the owner's own recording: never one that needs a credit, because there is no credits screen) are in `assets/audio/ambient/LICENSES.md`.
- The gain is the volume squared times `MAX_AMBIENT_GAIN` (0.17: about 15 dB under the voice at 100%; it was 0.35 until the step 27 walk-through, where the rain drowned the voice at 100%). The bed fades in over 600 ms when the voice is heard, out over 800 ms on a pause, the end or another devotional, dips for 250 ms before another sound, and never fades away from the foreground (JS timers stop there on Android). It plays only with the voice (Profile's preview excepted), at 1x whatever the devotional's speed, never on the lock screen, and never calls `setAudioModeAsync`.
- The bed follows the player store; a take stops it at once; `stop()` (sign-out) lets go of it; Off lets go of its player. A start or stop of the bed's own player that the app did not ask for (an interruption, or on Android a headset or car button that reached its media session) becomes a play or pause of the devotional after a 1.5-second grace; a start after a preview never starts a devotional.
- Now Playing's chips and volume save the choice as the default (`updateProfile`: a chip when pressed, the volume when the finger lifts). Profile changes only the default, and its Preview plays the default for 8 seconds while no devotional plays. Since 2026-11-20 a finished reflection also saves Gentle Piano at 100% as the default (`updateProfile`, from `services/newReflectionAmbient.ts`), so after a new reflection the default is Gentle Piano at 100% even when the person had chosen Off; like every sound it plays only under a devotional's voice, never by itself.
- Choosing or replacing a sound (step 27, 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-28): the user downloads it, under CC0, the Pixabay Content License or an own recording (Pixabay's API covers images and videos only, so a sound is never fetched at runtime), and the implementer makes the loop with prompt 27's script and records it in `assets/audio/ambient/LICENSES.md`; only that file and the loop change, no code. A rain recorded with drops close to the microphone fails the script's loudness rule ("more than 6 dB under the target"): three Pixabay rains were refused that way, and the rain kept is 3 dB softer than the piano and the birds for the same reason. The piano's source is Content ID registered, so it never goes in a video posted to YouTube (the user kept it, 2026-10-28; `LICENSES.md`, Notes); the rain's and the birds' sources are not registered.

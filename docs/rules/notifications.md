## Notification Rules (expo-notifications)

Built in step 28 (`src/services/affirmationPlanner.ts` works out the plan, `affirmationScheduler.ts` applies it, `notificationRouting.ts` handles the taps, and `notifications.ts` is the only file that imports `expo-notifications`). **Since 2026-11-17 the words come from the Word Deck** (`src/utils/wordDeck.ts`, pure): the three daily reminders carry the words of all the reflections of the week in turn, not only the newest reflection's three, and the deck is the rule that replaced step 28's "that slot's affirmation from the newest finished reflection". The rules below say what changed where it did.

- **Local notifications only.** Made on the phone from what it holds, with no push and no server: `enableRemoteNotifications: false` in `app.json`, so a build carries no iOS `aps-environment` entitlement and needs no Push capability. No exact-alarm permission on Android, so an affirmation there may arrive a few minutes late.
- **The Word Deck: what each reminder carries (2026-11-17).** Exactly three notifications a day, at the three reminder times, planned 7 days ahead (21 of iOS's 64), never more. The **pool** is every word (a headline with its verse) of the reflections of the last 7 local calendar days in the phone's zone, the newest 14 at most (`WORD_DECK_WEEK_DAYS`, `WORD_DECK_WEEK_MAX`), a heavy moment since 2026-11-08 included (its words speak hope); a week with fewer than 7 reflections is topped up with older ones to 7 (`WORD_DECK_MIN_REFLECTIONS`), so a quiet week still has variety; the phone's newest 40 cached entries are read (`WORD_DECK_LOOKBACK`). **The deal**, in time order: each reminder goes to the reflection that has waited longest for a turn (one never given a turn first, so a new reflection plays at the very next reminder; a tie goes to the week before older ones, then to the newer), and inside it to the word that has waited longest (a never-dealt word first, then the word whose own slot is the one being delivered, then the order: the model's best three in day order, then the added ones by position). A word never repeats until the others of its reflection have been heard, and a reflection is never given two turns in a row while another waits. It is a deterministic function of the pool and the calendar's history, so a run with nothing changed plans exactly what is scheduled, and a lone reflection with its three best words and no history reproduces step 28's plan (morning's word at the morning, and so on). Since gen-v2.7 (2026-11-23) every word a reflection gets fits any hour (the instruction's "Any hour of the day" and the `time_of_day` check, Pastoral Tone Rules), so a word's slot only says where it is sent first and the deal needs no change; words saved before gen-v2.7 keep their part of the day until they leave the pool. Reminder-time or time-zone changes and a new reflection deal the upcoming reminders again; the days already delivered are history and are never redone. **The calendar** (`word_calendar`, schema version 4, `src/db/wordCalendar.ts`) holds one row per reminder, `(day, slot)` with the instant, the entry id and the affirmation id, never a word: the rows ahead are replaced at each plan, a row at or before its time is history (what the rotation continues from, and what Today's card reads), 14 days are kept, and the file is deleted with the database at sign-out. It is written before anything is scheduled and a save is not a transaction (a save that stops half way only leaves reminders ahead without a row, and the next plan writes them). It is written whether or not notifications are available, allowed or switched on, so the card works with notifications off; only an unknown switch (the profile never read here), a closed database or an unreadable cache stops a run. **Today's card** shows the word of the reminder that fired last, within 26 hours (`WORD_CURRENT_MAX_AGE_MS`), from the calendar, and the same card and the lock screen therefore agree; with no row (a new phone, a calendar row older than a day, or a word the phone no longer holds) it shows the newest reflection's word for the current slot, as before. A tap on the card or the notification opens that word's own reflection on its Affirmations tab and never starts audio. **Reflections the phone has no affirmations for** (made before the deck, or on another phone) are fetched once in one request when Journal loads (`hydrateWordPool`, online only), before the plan is asked for again; a failed fetch leaves them out of the pool until the next one.
- **What is sent.** At each reminder time, the word the deck dealt for it. Since step 37 an affirmation with a verse is its headline as the title (bold on the lock screen) and the verse as the body, in curly quotes as one line, with its reference: “Because of the loving devotion of the LORD we are not consumed, for His mercies never fail.” — Lamentations 3:22 BSB (`copy.notifications.affirmationVerse`, the verse through `flowVerse` and `quoteVerse`). An affirmation without a verse (saved before step 37, or whose verse could not be found) keeps step 28's form: the title "{Slot} affirmation" (the Journal card's eyebrow words: "Morning affirmation", the slot being the one it is delivered at, which the deck may choose differently from the word's own) and the headline as the body. The verse is part of what an identifier hashes, so a reflection's notifications are replaced when its verses arrive. Never the affirmation's longer text, the person's words or the emotion. Sound on, no badge. Superseded on 2026-11-17: the 2026-11-09 rule that only the three affirmations carrying a lock-screen slot are sent. An affirmation the person added is a word of the deck like the others, heard after its reflection's best three (the model's slot is now only a preference for which reminder a word suits: the deck plays a word at the reminder whose slot it carries when it has the choice); the plan is made again after each addition. Its `data` carries `kind`, the entry id, the slot and the account's hash (the first 16 hex characters of SHA-256 of the Clerk user id, as step 24's file names), never words.
- **When nothing is sent.** The Daily Affirmations switch is off; the phone will not show notifications (iOS provisional delivery counts as allowed); the pool has no word; or the newest finished entry is a heavy moment saved before 2026-11-08, which has only the person's words and no devotional or affirmations: then nothing at all is sent, not even from an older reflection, until a newer reflection is ready (the pool's `quiet` flag). A word with no headline is left out; a verse with a missing half is sent as the headline alone. Since 2026-11-08 a heavy moment's affirmations are sent like any other's: they speak hope and God's presence, never the danger.
- **The window.** Today and the next 6 days at the reminder times (`AFFIRMATION_DAYS_AHEAD`, 21 notifications at most; iOS keeps 64), in the phone's current time zone (`src/utils/zonedTime.ts`, Intl only: a time the spring change skips fires at the first minute after it, and one the autumn change repeats fires the first time). Coming to the front refills the 7 days, so they stop by themselves if the app is not opened for 7 days.
- **Identifiers carry the plan.** `aff-{slot}-{yyyymmdd}-{hhmm}-{hash}`, the hash over the instant, the title, the body, the entry and the account. iOS reports a scheduled date trigger back as a time interval, so the identifiers are the schedule's memory for what is scheduled (the deck's own memory is the calendar, above): a run compares the planned identifiers with the scheduled ones and changes only the difference. It never cancels an identifier it did not plan (in Expo Go the scheduled list holds other projects' notifications too, so identifiers use neither `/` nor `\`), never touches one that is still wanted (one about to fire is kept), and adds a missing one only when it is at least 10 seconds ahead (`AFFIRMATION_MIN_LEAD_MS`: iOS refuses a time that has passed).
- **A run that cannot read something it needs changes nothing**: the user's database is not open, the profile was never read on this phone (the switch is unknown), or the cache or the calendar cannot be read (a bad read must not look like "no reflection", which would cancel the notifications). A reflection still being prepared never counts.
- **When it plans again.** At sign-in (once the database is open and the profile has been read on this phone), whenever the app comes to the front, when a reminder time changes (Profile, onboarding, or another phone through the profile sync), when the Daily Affirmations switch changes, when a reflection finishes (the sync engine's `finished` event, a heavy moment included), when Journal's first page and the newest reflection's affirmations have arrived from the server and the pool's reflections with no cached affirmations have been fetched (only then, since the step 28 follow-up: before them the plan would find no headlines), and after Profile's "Allow notifications". Today's card reads the calendar again each time a run has written it (also while another screen covers Today) and each time Today is shown (2026-11-18). Requests within half a second are one run (`AFFIRMATION_RESCHEDULE_DEBOUNCE_MS`), one run at a time.
- **Taps** open `Reflection` with `{ entryId, tab: "affirmations" }` once the signed-in screens are mounted and the account is known (Navigation Map, "Notification taps"). A tap on another account's notification opens nothing, and a tap never starts playback or the ambient sound.
- **While the app is open** a notification shows as a banner and in the list, quietly: no sound and no badge (`setNotificationHandler`).
- **Deleting a reflection (step 29b)** plans the notifications again once the phone has forgotten the entry (`requestAffirmationReschedule()`): its words leave the pool and the deck deals the upcoming reminders again from the reflections that remain (a reminder already delivered stays in the calendar as history, and the card shows no word of a deleted reflection: the hook drops it at once), and when none is left nothing is scheduled. Removing a prayer from Prayers changes nothing here. A heavy moment saved before 2026-11-08, when deleted, is treated the same: the quiet it imposed ends, and the notifications come from the reflection before it.
- **Sign-out and account deletion** (`signOutCleanup`) stop the planning, wait for a run in the air, cancel every scheduled notification whose identifier starts with `aff-` and remove those still in the notification center. When a session ends without a sign-out, what is scheduled stays until the next account's first plan replaces it.
- **Never** call `cancelAllScheduledNotificationsAsync()` or `dismissAllNotificationsAsync()`: they would wipe notifications the app did not make. The server columns `daily_affirmations.scheduled_for` and `is_delivered` stay unused (Database Rules).
- **Logs.** Only a step that failed (`[affirmations] {step}_failed`, development only) and the step 07 warnings: never a title, a headline, an identifier, an entry id, a user id or its hash, or a time.
- **The trial-end reminder (2026-11-18).** The one local notification that is not an affirmation. A person who starts a free trial on the Paywall may switch on "Remind me before my trial ends" (Screen specification 8; on by default), and the purchase then schedules a single notification (`services/trialReminder.ts`; the plan and the words are the pure `utils/trialReminder.ts`) at `TRIAL_REMINDER_AT` (10:00) on the person's own clock, `TRIAL_REMINDER_DAYS_BEFORE` (2) calendar days before the day the trial ends. That is 38 to 62 hours before the end (an hour more or less where the clocks change), so about a day is left to act on it: Apple lets a trial be cancelled up to 24 hours before it ends and no later. The words are value-first and not a cancel prompt (the owner's rule, 2026-11-18, after the first wording told people how to avoid being charged: a reminder that teaches people to cancel works against the subscription, and the large apps remind with what the person keeps and the price they will pay, without nagging): the title is "Your GraceHush trial ends Monday" (the weekday the trial ends in the person's own zone) and the body is "Keep unlimited devotionals, and Scholar opens when your plan begins. After your trial, Pro continues at $125.99 per year. Tap to manage your plan." (since 2026-11-18; it began "Keep unlimited devotionals and longer Scholar conversations." until the trial stopped including Scholar), the same on iPhone and Android. It names what the person keeps (the unlimited devotionals) and what opens when the plan begins (Scholar, which the trial does not include); the price and the period are the purchased package's own `priceString` and `periodLabel`, handed over by the Paywall (never typed in code, never another plan's: the monthly plan reads "$14.99 per month"), and with no price to name there is no reminder (`skipped`), because it never says a plan continues without saying at what. It says nothing of cancelling, being charged, a deadline, a clock time or a store, makes no promise and uses no pressure; the tap opens Profile, where Manage subscription is, and the Paywall's own disclosure still names where to cancel, as both stores require. The words are built by hand from the zone's own calendar, with no plan name, id or account in them, no words of the person's, no straight apostrophe and no narrow no-break space. Nothing about it is reported anywhere: no analytics event, and in development the log says a failed step and nothing else. Its identifier is `trial-ending` and the developer button's test is `trial-test-{ms}`: everything of ours starts with `trial-`, so a clean-up never touches an affirmation (`aff-…`) or another project's notification, and `cancelAllScheduledNotificationsAsync()` stays banned. It is scheduled only when the trial ends in the future, its reminder moment is at least `TRIAL_REMINDER_MIN_LEAD_MS` (10 s) ahead (iOS refuses a time that has passed) and the trial is at least `TRIAL_REMINDER_MIN_TRIAL_DAYS` (3) long (the launch trial is exactly three days, so its reminder comes at 10:00 the day after the purchase, within a day and a half of it and 37 to 63 hours before the end; a check holds this for purchases every 61 minutes through 2026 in four zones); a call replaces any earlier reminder; a permission never asked is asked once, then, in context, and a refusal is a calm line on the done view, never an error. The end comes from RevenueCat (`trialStatusFromCustomerInfo`: an active `pro` in its trial period with an expiration date; the entitlement store holds `trial: { endsAt, willRenew }`, cleared at sign-out and for another account), and the mock data source's trial ends when its three days are over (seven until 2026-11-18). The plan watch follows RevenueCat's answer for the signed-in account, on the real data source only and once for each answer (`reconcileTrialReminder`): a trial that is gone, or that was cancelled in the store and will not renew, has its waiting reminder removed and a shown one dismissed, and only that reminder (`trial-ending`): the developer panel's test notification (`trial-test-…`) is not a reminder for this account's trial and stays until it is tapped or cleared (found in the walk-through: a pretend trial's notification on an account with no real trial was dismissed at the next foreground); an active trial that renews is left alone with no call at all, and the reconcile never creates a reminder and never asks for a permission. Sign-out and account deletion, and the panel's Clear button, remove every `trial-` notification of ours, right after the affirmations (`signOutCleanup`). A tap opens the Profile tab (`MainTabs` with `pop: true`, where the plan and Manage subscription are), is held until the signed-in screens are up and acted on once, and never starts audio. Known limits: it lives on this phone only, so a new phone, a reinstall, or a trial bought on another phone has none (the store's own receipts and emails say when a trial ends); on Android it shares the "Daily affirmations" channel, so turning that channel off in system settings also silences it; iOS cannot report when a pending notification will fire, so the app trusts the date it scheduled; a trial cancelled in the store is noticed at the next RevenueCat answer (a foreground), not at the moment of cancelling.
- There is no "your reflection is ready" notification in V1 (decided in the step 11 follow-up, 2026-10-20; it answers manual setup item 4 of prompt 11). A local notification cannot know when the server finishes, so it would need a server push, which needs the Apple developer account (last, see Instructional Memory) and does not run in Expo Go on Android. The processing copy only promises that the reflection will be "waiting in your Journal". Revisit if step 19 makes processing asynchronous (Backend / Edge Function Rules, item 12).

# A quiet place for your heart.

GraceHush is a private audio prayer journal. Speak or write what you are carrying, and GraceHush gives you a passage from the Berean Standard Bible, a short spoken devotional, a closing prayer and three gentle affirmations, written from your own words.

- [Support](/support/)

GraceHush is not a crisis, medical or counseling service. If you are in danger, call 911. In the US you can also call or text 988 at any time.

# Ambient loops

The three sounds the Ambient Soundscape chips play under a devotional (step 27). Each loop was made from the source
below with the step 27 loop script: 75 seconds of whole AAC frames whose first 3 seconds cross-fade into the end, one
constant gain to the spoken devotional's loudness (-22 LUFS, or lower to keep the true peak under -1.5 dBTP), AAC-LC
128 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo, no metadata. None of these licenses needs a credit in the app.

## How each loop was made

| Sound | Source file | Start (s) | Length (s) | Fade (s) | Loudness (LUFS) | True peak (dBTP) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| soft-rain | spinopel-light-rain-321443.mp3 | 5 | 75 | 3 | -25.26 | -1.29 |
| gentle-piano | leberch-soft-piano-586714.mp3 | 45 | 75 | 3 | -22.05 | -3.86 |
| morning-birds | freesound_community-birds-19624.mp3 | 30 | 75 | 3 | -22.04 | -6.8 |

## Notes

- **gentle-piano:** its source page says "Content ID Registered" (YouTube's system that recognises a sound in uploaded
  videos by the sound itself, whatever it is called). That does not touch the app, but never use this sound in a video
  posted to YouTube (a promo, a tutorial, a store preview uploaded there): show Soft Rain, Morning Birds or Off instead.
  The rain's and the birds' pages show no Content ID registration (checked on 2026-10-28).
